questions - 
Why does skin colour change with your heartbeat?
Why the green channel and not red or blue?
If the signal goes up and down 1.5 times per second, what’s the heart rate?

answers-
Skin color changes because each heartbeat pushes a fresh surge of blood into facial capillaries, increasing light absorption, while the skin reflects more light between beats.
The green channel is used because green light penetrates to the exact skin depth where capillaries sit and is highly absorbed by hemoglobin, whereas red penetrates too deeply and blue not deep enough.
The heart rate is 90 BPM (1.5 beats per second multiplied by 60 seconds).
